# 2026-09-29 — Units in address entry

## Identify the right meter without sending its unit to Maps

Fill addresses and Add/Edit now accept `14-a` or `13-2` in the house-number box.
The first becomes **14 Whatever Lane · Unit a** on worklist, Drive, route-map,
and planner labels. Descriptive details such as `14-rear door` work too. The
base address and detail use the existing `address` and `unit` fields, so two
orders at one building stay distinguishable through syncing and capture.

Maps and route geocoding still receive only the base address (or the trusted
map pin). The clipboard includes the full label. Back/Edit restore the
shorthand; removing its suffix clears the unit. A unit-only edit preserves the
pin, and choosing a town also saves the unit currently in the number box.

**Trap:** units belong outside the navigable address. Do not rewrite historical
address strings in bulk or clear a pin merely because a unit changed. Cached
modules can differ in age: optional helper exports keep boot working, the
walkthrough checks whether its caller accepts units before splitting, and an
omitted unit argument preserves existing data. No Sheet/database migration or
service-worker change; phones update through Settings → Force update from GitHub.

**Verification:** the Node suite plus headless Edge checks of Save/Back/Edit,
unit clearing, navigation preferences and clipboard, capture prefill, Drive/route
labels, phone/planner sync, reload, town selection, geocoding, narrow phone layout,
and old HTML/address-module/worklist-module combinations. All external requests
were intercepted; no production Sheet writes.
