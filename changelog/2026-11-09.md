# 2026-11-09 — Timed appointments stay put after a "start from here" Optimize

## The re-schedule after Optimize starts from where Optimize did

**The report:** Optimize put both of the day's timed appointments (10:30 and 13:00) in
their proper slots. Opening the route map, or pressing 🚗 Drive, then moved both to the
top of the list. Stop 1 showed an ETA of 13:24, 174 min late, and stop 2 was 44 min late,
at 08:50 with the whole day still ahead.

**The cause:** the screens around Optimize re-time the day passively: coming back from
the map, the Drive screen's refresh, and boot all run `applyTodayAnchor`. That prices
Day 1's drive out from `estimateTravel`'s origin ladder: live GPS fix, then the last
completed stop, then the **crew's muster point**. The Optimize had been run "from here".
With no fresh GPS fix (the recorder adds no points while the truck sits still) and
nothing logged yet, the ladder fell to the muster point, which for this crew is Norwich,
about 150 km from a day in Corunna. Day 1's first leg became a four-hour drive, so no
arrangement of the day was on time. `placeAppointments` then did what it does when every
arrangement is late: it took the least-late one, which puts every appointment first.

**What shipped:** a rung in the ladder, between the live fix and the last completed stop:
the spot the last Optimize started "from here" (`runStartPin`). It is the phone-only
`wlRouteStartFix` the route map already draws its start pin from (2026-11-05), valid by
the same rule (`liveStartFix`): the same plan day, and no stop logged since. After the
first log, the last completed stop takes over as before. A morning-meet Optimize saves no
spot, so it is unchanged.

**Verified:** the report was reproduced in Node and on the real page in headless
Chromium. The page was seeded with 20 orders near Corunna, a Norwich muster point, a
saved "start from here" spot and the clock at 08:50, and the worklist was opened. On the
shipped code the 10:30 went to slot 1, 132 min late. With the fix it stays at slot 5, on
time.

**The trap:** this was not new code in the map. The map change only *saved* the GPS
start, for drawing. Every re-time still ignored it, so a "from here" route was only
correct until the next passive re-schedule. Any new reader of "where does the crew start
today" has to go through `estimateTravel`, or the map and the ETAs tell two stories again.
