# 2026-11-27 — A tie between two towns is picked on a map, never guessed; the Drive screen times how long you are at a house

## ⚠ pick a town always asks, and 🗺 Map shows where each town is

**The problem:** when the offline map found the same street and number in two towns, the
app took the town nearest the phone without asking, if it was at least twice as close as
the other (the 2026-11-18 rule). The owner wants to choose, and to see where each answer
is before choosing.

**What shipped:**

- **Nothing is auto-picked.** `pickBest` in `js/route.js` no longer settles a tie. Every
  candidate gets `km` (its distance from the Optimize gate centre, which is the phone's
  GPS) and the nearest is marked `near: true`. The district index lists them
  nearest-first; Google and ORS keep their own order.
- **Every town chip row starts with 🗺 Map, and the nearest town is marked ★ nearest.**
  That covers the card, the Edit form and the address screen. Map opens
  `js/town-picker.js`: a sheet with each town as a lettered pin, the nearest one in orange,
  a blue "You" dot when the phone has a position, and one button per town. A tap on a pin
  or a button pins the order through the same `pickTown` as a chip.
- **Use nearest town for all.** A banner at the top of the list counts the orders waiting
  on a town and pins each to its ★ nearest in one tap (`pickNearestAll`). An order with
  no ★ is left and counted in the toast.

**Worth knowing:**

- The 2026-11-18 rule existed because, without it, about **45% of offline lookups** asked
  (crew 5 km out, published district). The owner chose to have every tie ask anyway,
  with the banner as the one-tap way to take the nearest for all.
- The highlight comes from `near`, not the live GPS fix, so the chip, the map and the
  banner can't disagree. Only an order looked up before today, with no `near`, is
  measured from the live fix on the map; the banner leaves it alone.
- `town-picker.js` is a dynamic `import()` and is not in the `sw.js` SHELL, like the
  activity log, so `sw.js` is untouched. A phone that can't load it still has the chips.
  The sheet builds its own markup, so there is no new id in `index.html` for an old cached
  page to miss.
- Nothing here uploads. The picks go up with the next log or ⇪ Upload, like every other
  edit on the worklist screen.

## Parked and Leaving: a timer for how long the truck is at a house

**The problem:** the app had no direct measure of time at a house. It was worked out
from the gaps between logs and from parked holes in the GPS track. The owner wants the
crew to mark it themselves, and asked for collection only: nothing is to use the
numbers until there is data to look at.

**What shipped:**

- **Two buttons on the Drive screen**, either side of "2 of 14". **Parked** starts a
  timer and **Leaving** ends it. The line under them names the order being timed, and
  after Leaving it shows what was saved.
- **Each timer is one row on a new `SiteVisits` tab** (`saveSiteVisit` in `Code.gs`),
  the twentieth tab. It makes itself on the first write. Nothing reads it.
- **A forgotten Leaving does not block anything.** Parked at the next house ends the
  old timer (`endedBy: nextStart`) and starts the new one. The row carries both
  orders, and the estimated drive between them is taken off: `minutes` stays the raw
  figure, `driveMin` is the drive, `onSiteMin` is the difference. `navTime` is when
  Navigate was first pressed on another order while the timer ran.
- **It uploads through the offline queue at the press.** The owner first asked for the
  3-minute Drive refresh to carry it. That refresh runs only while tracking is armed,
  driving stats are on and the Drive screen is in front, so on a day without tracking
  it never runs. He chose the queue.
- **Small rules:** a timer ended under 30 seconds is cancelled as a mis-tap, and one
  still running the next day is saved with no end time.

**Traps worth remembering:**

- **Navigate moves the big card on to the next order**, so the house the truck is
  parked at is the "Driving to" card, not the big one. The first browser run also
  showed that a double tap on Parked jumped the timer to the next order and threw the
  first one away as too short; a second press inside 30 seconds now does nothing.
- **A Leaving press must not read the big card.** When the order is logged and the list
  refreshes, the card moves on while the truck is still in the driveway, which would
  read as a forgotten timer and take a drive off a visit that had none.
- **The drive taken off is one leg.** A timer forgotten through several houses loses
  only the drive from its first order to its last. The raw minutes are kept so this
  can be redone from the sheet.
- **No markup and no stylesheet rule were added.** The row is built in `js/drive.js`
  with inline styles, and `js/site-visit.js` is a dynamic `import()` that is not in the
  `sw.js` SHELL. A phone on a new `drive.js` and an older page still gets a working
  row, and one that cannot load the module gets the old screen.
