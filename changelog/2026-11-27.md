# 2026-11-27 — A tie between two towns is picked on a map, never guessed

## ⚠ pick a town always asks, and 🗺 Map shows where each town is

**The problem:** when the offline map found the same street and number in two towns, the
app took the town nearest the phone without asking, if it was at least twice as close as
the other (the 2026-11-18 rule). The owner wants to choose, and to see where each answer
is before choosing.

**What shipped:**

- **Nothing is auto-picked.** `pickBest` in `js/route.js` no longer settles a tie. Every
  candidate gets `km` (its distance from the Optimize gate centre, which is the phone's
  GPS) and the nearest is marked `near: true`. The district index lists them
  nearest-first; Google and ORS keep their own order.
- **Every town chip row starts with 🗺 Map, and the nearest town is marked ★ nearest.**
  That covers the card, the Edit form and the address screen. Map opens
  `js/town-picker.js`: a sheet with each town as a lettered pin, the nearest one in orange,
  a blue "You" dot when the phone has a position, and one button per town. A tap on a pin
  or a button pins the order through the same `pickTown` as a chip.
- **Use nearest town for all.** A banner at the top of the list counts the orders waiting
  on a town and pins each to its ★ nearest in one tap (`pickNearestAll`). An order with
  no ★ is left and counted in the toast.

**Worth knowing:**

- The 2026-11-18 rule existed because, without it, about **45% of offline lookups** asked
  (crew 5 km out, published district). The owner chose to have every tie ask anyway,
  with the banner as the one-tap way to take the nearest for all.
- The highlight comes from `near`, not the live GPS fix, so the chip, the map and the
  banner can't disagree. Only an order looked up before today, with no `near`, is
  measured from the live fix on the map; the banner leaves it alone.
- `town-picker.js` is a dynamic `import()` and is not in the `sw.js` SHELL, like the
  activity log, so `sw.js` is untouched. A phone that can't load it still has the chips.
  The sheet builds its own markup, so there is no new id in `index.html` for an old cached
  page to miss.
- Nothing here uploads. The picks go up with the next log or ⇪ Upload, like every other
  edit on the worklist screen.
