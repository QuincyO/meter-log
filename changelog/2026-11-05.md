# 2026-11-05 — The route map: split pins, a steady zoom, and the real start

## Stops at one address share a split pin

**The report:** on the route map, stops at one house sat on top of each other. In the
field screenshot, 1 was hidden under 2, 3 couldn't be seen, and 6 was half under 7. The
crew couldn't tell how many stops were at a spot, and a tap only reached the top one.

**What shipped:** stops on the same pin, or at the same street address within 50 m, now
share one circle split into a section per stop, each showing its route number. Five or
more show a count (`×8`). Tapping any pin opens a list with the work order, unit and ETA,
and ▲▼ reorder the units at that pin. A move swaps two stops and nothing else, so a pin
holding 3 and 9 trades them and 4 to 8 stay put. It saves the same way as dragging a card.
The route line still runs through each stop's own coordinates.

**The trap:** the first version bound a popup to each marker. Markers are rebuilt on every
redraw, so a close and a reopen could overlap and leave two lists on screen. The test
screenshots hid it, because the delay they add was enough to dodge the race. It is one
shared popup on the map now, and a redraw only swaps its content.

## The route map keeps its zoom when you reorder

**The report:** every reorder zoomed the map back out, and a far-away morning meet made
that worse. The zoom-out on opening the map was fine.

**What shipped:** the map now fits only when it opens or when the day changes.

**The trap:** skipping the fit on the reorder path wouldn't have worked. A reorder reaches
`refresh()` twice, the second time through `planAdvance` → `renderWorklist`, so the fit
is opt-in instead. Overlapping redraws could also leave a second set of pins, so an older
redraw now stops if a newer one has started.

## Optimizing from where you are shows that spot as the start

**The report:** optimizing from the current location still showed the morning meet.

**The cause:** the route was solved from GPS, but the saved drive-out line, which is where
the map reads its start pin from, was always built from the crew start.

**What shipped:** Day 1 now starts at the GPS point until the next stop is logged. Later
days keep the morning meet, because that's where those mornings begin.

**The trap:** the obvious fix was to build the saved line from GPS. That line syncs to the
`Worklist` tab, and the `worklist` read isn't admin-gated, so it would have published
where the installer stood, and broken the guide's promise that the location isn't
uploaded. The point stays on the phone in `wlRouteStartFix`. Other devices and the planner
still show the morning meet.
