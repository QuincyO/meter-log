# 2026-11-03 — After an appointment, the route carries on from it

## The stops after an appointment or a lock are the ones nearest it

**The report:** a 17-stop day on the West St cluster had a timed appointment across town
at slot 6. After it, slots 7–17 drove straight back to West St, while orders a block from
the appointment were on later days. The owner asked whether Optimize uses the drive
between every pair of addresses. It does: the road matrix is all-pairs, from the
district pack or Google. But the scheduler only used it for ETAs and for picking the
appointment's slot.

**Cause:** same shape as [yesterday's slot-1 lock](2026-11-02.md). The route is solved
with appointments and locks left out. `scheduleRouteConstraints` then drops each one
into its slot, and the free slots around it take the next stops of that sequence. That
sequence never knew the appointment was there.

**What shipped:** reflow. Both Optimize handlers pass `opts.reflow`, built by
`route.js anchorFiller` over the run's distance matrix. `route-constraints.js fillDay`
fills each run of free slots that follows an anchor from that anchor:
- before another anchor: cheapest insertion into the path between the two;
- after the day's last anchor: nearest-neighbour, with the end bent home by the commute
  pull.

It draws on every order still unplaced, whatever day it was tagged for. The owner
re-plans today every morning, so the later days don't matter. Days keep their target
size. The stretch before the first anchor, and the one after the slot-1 lead, keep the
solver's order. A 🔒 locked work list restricts Day 1 to its frozen members. The phone
now applies that lock before scheduling, as the planner already did, so
`applyTodayAnchor` doesn't re-shuffle the result. `placeAppointments` searches slots
with the same `fillDay` (new `fillSlots` argument), so it prices the day that will
really be driven.

**The trap:** reflow is Optimize-only. A drag or the per-log re-anchor must never pass
it, or the installer's own order is re-shuffled under them. And the slot search and the
final day must use one fill, or an on-time slot can arrive late (AGENTS.md has both).
The pages reach `anchorFiller` through `import * as routeMod` with a `typeof` check, so
a stale cached `route.js` costs the reflow, not the page.

**Verification:** new `tests/route-reflow.test.mjs` (11 tests). Its fixture is a
15-stop cluster, an anchor ~1.5 km off and six orders by the anchor. Without reflow,
slot 7 goes back to the cluster, which reproduces the report. With it, slots 7–12 are
the six orders by the anchor, nearest first. The same holds for a timed appointment,
which stays on time. Orders by the anchor that were tagged Day 2 move into today. Two
anchors get the stops on the way between them. A 🔒 day keeps its members. The lead's
order is untouched. No anchors means no change. A bad filler answer can't drop or
duplicate an order. It also runs the real `optimizeRoute` → scheduler pipeline. The
full suite passes (794). Timing on random lists: 120 orders with 3 appointments took
127 ms with reflow (16 ms without); 200 orders with 3 took 155 ms. A headless Edge
load of `index.html` with the spine stubbed shows no exceptions, and both new exports
resolve. A real Optimize tap on a road pack was not driven.
