# 2026-06-28 — Timestamps stop lying; the PDF moves to the phone

`4e6e5bf`, `d2a7498`

A close reproduction of the paper template, drawn on-device with a vendored jsPDF. Generate
and Close now work with **no signal** — offline it builds the summary from the cached day —
and there's no Drive archive round-trip.

## Two timestamp bugs that had been corrupting morning data

**UTC "Z time" killed.** Sheet Date cells are normalized to naive Toronto strings. `d6c4c4c`

**Single-digit hours tolerated everywhere.** Some rows store `9:13:44`, not `09:13:44` — the
frontend emitted an unpadded hour in some engines, and those rows stay that way forever. This
broke morning data twice over: parsers requiring two digits returned null and dropped *every*
pre-10:00 stop from the gap and travel math, and comparing the raw timestamp as text sorted
`8:52` after `11:11`, pushing morning work-order cards to the end of the list. All hour
parsers take `\d{1,2}` now and card sorts parse the value instead of comparing strings.
`2fd3c14`, `b0059f0`

**Never lexicographically compare a timestamp here, and assume the hour may be one digit.**
