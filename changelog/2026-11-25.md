# 2026-11-25 — A picked gap on the audit timeline says which order it runs from and to; the daily log says which downtime was before the first order and after the last, and both can carry a note

## The gap detail and the timeline read "this order → that order"

**The problem:** tapping a block on a day's timeline opened the gap's detail under a line
that read `#14 1028831 (11:30, …) to #15 1024729 (12:31, …)`, and the bar itself only
outlined the picked blocks in black. The outline drew a seam between every block inside
the gap too, and nothing on the bar said which edge was the earlier order's log and which
was the next one's.

**What shipped:**

- **The detail opens with two order cards and an arrow between them**: the earlier order on
  the left, the next on the right, each with its time and address. On a phone they stack and
  the arrow points down. A screen reader still hears "to" (`gapDetailHtml`).
- **The picked gap is bracketed on the bar, labelled `#14 11:30 → #15 12:31`.** The
  bracket's left end sits on the earlier order's log and its right end on the next one's;
  the rest of the bar fades. `timelineHtml` draws one hidden bracket per gap, placed from the
  same blocks the bar is drawn from, and the page shows the one it picks (`openGap`).
  Near either end of the bar the label hangs inward, as the clock marks under it do.

**Worth knowing:**

- **Page only.** The PDF draws the timeline from `timelineBlocks()` and has no gap detail, so
  it is unchanged; print hides the bracket.
- **The brackets' positions are CSS custom properties (`--l`/`--w`), not widths**, so nothing
  that adds up the bar's widths (the tests' 100 % check) counts them.
- **No new export** crosses a module, so a new `audit-view.js` beside an old cached
  `audit-charts.js` or `audit-render.js` still links (AGENTS.md, the Audits pages, item 20):
  an old chart module simply has no bracket to show.

## The gap label stays on a narrow phone, and an old stylesheet no longer shows every label

**The problem:** two display faults in the change above, found by driving the pages after
it was merged. On a phone up to 390 px wide, the label of a late-afternoon gap ran past the
screen edge (by up to 10 px at 320 px) and the page scrolled sideways. And beside a stylesheet
from before the brackets, every gap's label showed at once: 31 lines above the bar on a
33-order day.

**What shipped:**

- **On a phone the label hangs inward from 30 % of the bar at either end**, not 12 %. The
  12 % was the clock marks' figure, and this label is over half as wide as a phone's bar. A
  wider screen still centres it as before (`gapMarkers`, classes `pl`/`pr`, used only in the
  640 px block).
- **Each bracket carries the `hidden` attribute**, which `.au-tlsel.sel` overrides. With an
  older stylesheet a picked gap has no bracket, as it did before brackets existed.

**Worth knowing:**

- **The skew check covers stylesheets too.** "No new export crosses a module" keeps a mix of
  old and new scripts working; it says nothing about new markup that only the new CSS hides.
- **Under mobile emulation, `innerWidth` grows with the content**, so a check of
  `scrollWidth <= innerWidth` passes on a page that scrolls sideways. Compare with the width
  you set.

## The land daily log names the before-first and after-last downtime

**The problem:** downtime from End of day's **Before first order** and **After last order**
cards printed as one "Not tied to a WO#" line, mixed with any downtime added with no work
order. The sheet did not say which was which.

**What shipped:** up to three lines under the totals row, each printed only when it has
minutes: **Before first work order**, **After last work order**, and **Not tied to a WO#**
for the rest. Column totals are unchanged (`untiedLines` in `js/dailylog.js`).

**Worth knowing:**

- **Told apart by the gap key**, `00:00–00:00` and `23:59–23:59`, the same two
  `capture.js edgeOf` reads. A past day reprints the new way with no data change.
- **The lines now start a new page when they do not fit.** The one line was drawn wherever
  the totals row left it; three could run off the bottom.
- Land sheet only. Breaks and misc travel still do not print on it.

## A note on Before first order and After last order

**The problem:** an order's card has a note, and the two no-order cards had nowhere to say
why the time was lost.

**What shipped:** each card in End of day has **▸ Add a note**, and the office editor has a
note box on each. It prints in the PDF's Notes block, right after the end-of-day note, as
"Before first work order" or "After last work order". No minutes are needed.

**Worth knowing:**

- **It is kept on the day's `Days` row** (`beforeNote`, `afterNote`), not in the `Downtime`
  row, whose note is the gap key. The new `saveDayNotes` adds the columns itself, so there
  is no `setupSheets()` to run.
- **It is not part of `saveDay`.** That writes both bookend times every time, and a note is
  saved as it is typed, so a note at noon would have filed 7:30–4:00 as the day's hours.
- **The queue is the only record of an unsent note** (`pendingDayNotes`). A stash on the
  cached day would be dropped by a phone running an older `daycache.js`.
- The audit does not show it.
