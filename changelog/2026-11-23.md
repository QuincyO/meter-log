# 2026-11-23 — The audit PDF's route map is zoomed to the orders; a day's audit is something to skim; a typed town is found from the streets around it; Clear map pins

## The route map in the PDF fits the orders alone

**The problem:** the map under **Route worked** in a day's audit PDF was zoomed to hold
everything drawn on it: the orders, the line between them and, with **Show drive log**
ticked, the whole drive. A drive out from the start location zoomed it out until the
numbered pins bunched together and the order worked could not be read.

**What shipped:** the box is fitted to the orders' pins and nothing else. The drive log and
the road line are still drawn, and are cut off at the edge of the box where they go
further. The street map behind it covers the same area.

**Traps worth remembering:**

- The fit and the clip go together. A line that leaves the box is drawn over the page's
  text unless it is clipped.
- The drawer and the street-map fetch must read the same points (`mapParts`), or the pins
  and the picture drift apart.
- The map on the audit page is not changed. Ticking **Show drive log** there still zooms
  out to the drive.

## A day's audit is something to skim, with the reasons on the gaps

**The ask, from the owner after reading his PDF:** it was too many words, and technical
ones. A supervisor should be able to look at the charts, read the headlines and a few
numbers, and know how the day went. His notes should sit on the gaps they explain, and
every gap should carry its work order number.

**What shipped**, on the day view of both audit pages and in its PDF:

- **The day at a glance:** six tiles, the comment typed at End of day, and one caption.
- **Where the time went:** a row for each gap that ran long or had something entered on
  it, with the order number, the minutes over usual, what was entered, and the order's
  note or UTI reason under it.
- **Every order:** shorter, with the UTI reason in the status and any note not already
  shown above.
- How the figures are worked out is four closed sections at the bottom of the page. They
  are not in the PDF. A 33-order day went from four pages to two.
- The PDF no longer has speeds. The page keeps them.
- "Longest pause" names its order on the period view too. Otherwise the period view is
  unchanged.

**The read carries more:** `?action=audit` now returns order notes, UTI reasons and End of
day comments. It is token-only. The notes were already readable that way through `day`; the
comment was only in the gated `tracker` read, and the owner was told before it was added.

**Traps worth remembering:**

- The comment is on the Tracker row, so it exists only after the day is closed.
- Notes print as typed. Anything in one goes to whoever is sent the PDF.
- No number changed: the out-of-repo figure check still reads 0 DIFF.
- The expected number keeps its conditions on screen: whose pace it is, that it is not a
  target, and how close it has been.

## A typed town is found from the streets around it

**The report, from the owner:** six orders typed `6 York St, Delaware, ON N0L 1E0` (and
16, 15, 14, 10, 8) were put on York St in London. He was in London when he pressed
Optimize.

**The cause:** the downloaded map tags each address point with whatever town the mapper
wrote. York St's points in Delaware say "Middlesex Centre", the municipality. Gideon Dr
and Longwoods Rd, beside it, say "Delaware". The lookup read the town only from the
candidate road's own points, found no York St tagged Delaware, and dropped the typed town.
That left a tie between 19 York Streets, and a tie goes to the one clearly nearest the
phone: London's. The saved route had a 19.4 km leg out to the first York St order.

**What shipped:** when no candidate road carries the typed town, the lookup finds that
town's points on other streets and keeps the candidates within 3 km of one
(`besideTown`, `TOWN_NEAR_M` in `js/roadgraph.js`). York St in Delaware is 90 m from a
point tagged Delaware; York St in London is 13 km from one. Run against the published
district with the owner's 117 orders: the six York St orders move from London to
Delaware and the other 110 lookups are unchanged.

**Traps worth remembering:**

- The postal code is not read. The pack holds none, so `N0L 1E0` on the end neither
  helps nor hurts. The town is what narrows it.
- It runs only when no candidate carries the tag itself, so a road that is tagged with
  the typed town is found exactly as before.
- With no town typed, a tie goes to the street nearest the phone, not nearest the rest
  of the list. That is the owner's rule, confirmed on 2026-11-23. The user guide said
  "nearest your other orders" and now says "nearest your phone".
- An order already pinned in the wrong town is not looked up again, because its pin is
  inside the radius. Edit the address and save it to clear the pin, then Optimize.
- After a wrong pin, the town-writing pass puts that pin's town on the address
  (`confirmFoundAddresses`): a wrong pin in London rewrites "Delaware" as "London".
- The 3 km comes from the published district: of the streets in a small town, 89% lie
  within 3 km of another street's point with the same tag, and 95% within 5 km.

**Also looked at, nothing changed in the app:** holding Optimize showed an error. Google's
Routes service answered every call with `403 PERMISSION_DENIED — Permission denied on the
service.` A geocode on the same key worked. The Routes API was not enabled on the
project; the owner enabled it the same day and a test call then returned a route
(DEPLOY.md, the optimize toast entry). With 85 orders the OpenRouteService backup is over
its size limit, so while Google refused, the hold fell to straight lines.

## 📍 Clear map pins

**The ask, from the owner:** a way to delete all the pins on the orders. A pin set in the
wrong town could not be fixed quickly in the field.

**Why it was slow:** a pin inside the radius is never looked up again. The only way to
drop one was to retype that order's address.

**What shipped:** a **📍 Clear map pins (N)** button near the bottom of the worklist. After
a confirm it removes the pin and the parked flags from every order still to do. The next
Optimize looks each address up again from where the phone is.

**Traps worth remembering:**

- Clearing the pin alone would not have fixed York St. After a wrong pin the app writes
  that pin's town onto the address, so "6 York St, Delaware" read "6 York St, London" and
  the next lookup would find London again. The app now keeps the text it writes a town
  over (`typedAddress`) and a clear puts it back.
- Orders rewritten before today have no typed copy. For those the clear takes the town
  off, and the nearest street of that name to the phone is used. So press it in the area
  being worked.
- An order that was never pinned keeps its text. A pin typed as coordinates and a logged
  order are never cleared.
- `typedAddress` stays on the phone. A list download would wipe it, so the download
  carries it over for each order whose address comes back unchanged.
- The town-writing pass runs behind Optimize. It now checks the order still has the same
  pin before it writes, or a clear pressed in that moment would get the old town back.
- Nothing is uploaded. Until the next log or ⇪ Upload, the Drive screen's refresh can put
  the sheet's pins back.
- After a clear, an address the downloaded map does not have is set aside on a tap. Hold
  Optimize to look those up online.
