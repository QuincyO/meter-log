# 2026-11-17 — A long photo-bot reply goes out in parts

## ✅ Send answered "message is too long" and the 🗑 button never came

**The problem:** at 8:23 a photo ✅ Send queued its orders. Then the bot answered
`Something went wrong: sendMessage: Bad Request: message is too long`. The reply after a
photo Send lists every worklist order that was not in the photos, one line each with
its address and appointment. The list ran past Telegram's 4096-character limit (around
55 orders or more), so Telegram refused the whole message. The 🗑 Remove button rides on
that message, so nothing could be taken off the worklist. Nothing was removed and the
queue was not affected. Tapping Send again did the same thing.

**What shipped:** `say` now sends a reply over the limit as several messages. It splits
at line breaks, so each order stays on one line, and puts the buttons on the last part,
under the end of the list they act on. This also covers `/list` on a very big batch.
The split is `splitMessage` in `extract.mjs`, where `node --test` can reach it.

**The trap:** Telegram doesn't shorten a long message, it refuses it outright. Any reply
built from a list can grow without bound, so it has to go through `say`, never a direct
`tg('sendMessage')`.
