# 2026-06-21 — Teams take shape; CI starts deploying

- **Per-boat member letter maps**, replacing the earlier flat crew list: crew sharing a letter
  on a boat are partners, and a boat renders as number + letter (`11A`). Captain and sub lists
  arrived alongside. `f79deb0`, `6821ad4`, `bd40e4b`
- **The teams page**: collapsible cards, crew search, member dropdown, and header-safe writes
  — writes that map onto the sheet's *actual* column order, so a reordered column can't
  scramble them. `514fa52`
- **CI auto-deploys `Code.gs` to Apps Script** via clasp on every push to `main`. This is why
  a spine change isn't live until it's pushed. `9ba3a42`
- Teams schema migration made tolerant: missing columns are auto-added, with a fallback to
  the legacy shape. `0703bfc`, `bfd7a36`
- **`ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CLAUDE.md` started here.** `bd40e4b`, `597e851`
