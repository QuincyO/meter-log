# 2026-06-23 — Travel time becomes per-installer

## Travel time, computed per installer

`ad5a8a8`, `9b2df41`, `c4ac706`

First per-workorder with downtime subtracted at end of day, then made per-installer rather
than team-wide, and finally computed from each installer's **own** consecutive meters. A
boat's two installers work different stops; averaging them together produced a number that
described neither.

## Dispatch downtime auto-calculated from meter requests

`39a277d`, `769187b` — and "Requested?" became a toggle button beside the Work order field.

## The edit page grew up

`15330bd`, `84e29b6`, `3f0fc49`

Manual day-close, PDF-only generate, per-log travel dropdowns, WO cards ordered
chronologically with each arrival time on its row, and the first work order's leg measured
from Departure.

## Daily log

`f5c250f`, `68ea8e2`, `fdc372a`

Trimmed to installs and UTIs with install-to-install timing; a PDF-only toggle to omit delays
and travel; and a fix for Departure/Returned cells printing dates instead of times.

## Analytics

`e4a5046`, `55490e0` — average time between meters, a typed multi-installer filter, and a
corrected install-to-install tile.
