# 2026-06-25 — Storage-first: a stop survives before it's sent

## The day log became storage-first

`86ad138`

Logging a stop writes the IndexedDB queue **and** updates the cached day immediately, so the
record exists on the phone before it ever reaches the Sheet. A server pull merges rather than
clobbers: server wins by id, still-pending local rows are overlaid.

## The end-of-day travel review works offline

`77d2540` — gaps derived from cached stop timestamps, with in-progress deductions stashed
locally until they sync.

## Dispatch averages

`73b9ec4`, `7cbe69a` — `avgDispatchTime()` pairs requested meters to completed installs and
persists the average to a `Metrics` tab. Same-day pairs are measured; cross-day pairs are
recorded but excluded from the mean, so an overnight wait doesn't skew it.
