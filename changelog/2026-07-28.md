# 2026-07-28 — The pace gauge can say "behind" again, a second phone finds out what the first one logged, and a route re-planned at noon stops departing at 8:15

One field report, two devices: the crew logs meters on a work phone and drives by a
second one. *"When I download the work list and get the orders that I've done, the pace
just sets the target to be the remaining metres — it always says I'm on pace. And when I
re-optimize it always begins from 8:15 and never takes into account what time it is now."*
Three separate defects behind it, all of them shipped here.

## "On pace" was a question that answered itself

`js/compute/estimate.js`, `js/drive.js`, `js/worklist.js`, `js/worklist-tuning.js`,
`tests/estimate.test.mjs`, `tests/pace-live.test.mjs`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `AGENTS.md`,
`USER-GUIDE.md`

`paceFor` decided the gauge's verdict with `onPace: (pendingCount - willDo) <= 0` —
*"will I finish the stops still on today's route?"* — and `js/drive.js` captioned it
`${done} of ${done + pendingCount} stops`.

**But today's route is Day 1, and Day 1 shrinks by exactly what has been done.**
`day1Count(anchor, day1Ids, dayCapacity(target, installedToday))` sizes it at
`target − installed`, so every completed meter took one stop off the numerator *and* one
off the denominator. The bar refilled itself no matter how the day was going, and
"behind" was not a state the gauge could reach. The installer's phrasing — "it just sets
the target to be the remaining metres" — was a precise description of the arithmetic.

The meters/day target is the only denominator that does not move underneath the answer,
so that is what `onPace` means now: `targetShort === 0`. The route shortfall survives as
`routeShort`, printed as the caption's second half ("· 2 stops won't fit"), because it is
the one thing the target cannot say — you can be on pace for 24 and still not reach every
house on the list. The bar clamps at 100%, since a day that beats its target (walk-ups,
or just a good one) is on pace and not an overflow.

**Trap worth remembering:** the meters/day number is returned **once, at the top level**
of `projectDayReal`. `paces.target` is already the finish-by *horizon*, so echoing it onto
each pace would have produced `paces.target.target` — two unrelated meanings of the word
one dot apart. `js/drive.js` reads `est.target`; the two only meet inside `fillPaceRow`,
where there is a comment saying so.

## The driving phone believed nothing had been installed all day

`js/worklist.js`, `tests/pace-live.test.mjs`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `AGENTS.md`,
`USER-GUIDE.md`

The other half, and the reason the report said the caption read **"0 of N"**. Everything
downstream of "how much has been done today" — `projectDayReal`'s `done`, `tallyOn`'s
`installed`, and therefore `dayCapacity` — reads `dayCache[`name|today`]`. That cache is
only ever filled by `cacheRecentDays`, which ran at page load and on reconnect.

**A phone the crew only drives by never logs anything, so nothing on it ever invalidates
that cache.** Open since morning, it saw zero installs until it was reloaded: `done = 0`
made the gauge structurally incapable of reporting a shortfall, and `installedToday = 0`
handed Day 1 a full fresh target. `drive.js` repainting the pace every four seconds just
re-read the same stale copy faster.

`wlDownload` now pulls today down (`cacheRecentDays(1)`, a single-date `range` call)
**before** `applyTodayAnchor` — a refresh after it would size the day off the stale copy
and defeat the point. `drivePace` refreshes on its own **3-minute** throttle, stamped
before the await so a slow call cannot stack up behind itself.

**The landmine:** the cache key is `${cfg().name}|${date}`. If the two phones' Settings ▸
name differ by so much as a space, none of this fires and the symptom is identical.
Check that first. It is now in the user guide as well, since it is the crew's to get right.

## A route re-planned at noon still departed at 8:15

`js/route-constraints.js`, `js/worklist.js`, `tests/route-constraints.test.mjs`,
`tests/route-depart.test.mjs`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `AGENTS.md`

`scheduleRouteConstraints` read `opts.firstStopTime` **once** and handed the same value to
`simulateDay` and `placeAppointments` for **every** day of the route. `ROUTE_DEPART_TIME`
is where the day starts — it is not where every re-solve of that day starts. Press
Optimize at 11:40 and every ETA on the route was a morning ETA, and the appointment
search was picking slots against a day that had already half happened.

`opts.day1FirstStopTime` overrides day 0 alone (`firstMinFor(day)`); days 2+ keep the
constant, and omitting it is byte-identical to the old behaviour — there is a test that
asserts exactly that, because it is the regression that would matter.

**The rule is about the anchor, not the hour**, and that came straight from the
installer: the muster point is a *morning* anchor, so an Optimize staged there keeps 08:15
however late it is pressed — that press means "show me the day as planned from the depot".
"Start from here" is a run happening *now*, and so is every rolling re-schedule. The
"Start from here" gate had already answered **where** a mid-day re-optimize begins;
nothing had ever answered **when**.

Two guard rails carried over from the finish-by clock that was scrapped on 2026-07-27:
the override reaches **ETAs only** — `counts[]`/`capFor`/`dayCapacity` are untouched, or
that dial is back under a new name — and it only ever moves the clock *forward*, so a run
before 08:15 keeps the constant rather than promising a 06:40 start nobody is driving.
`wlDayEta` takes the same clock for day 1, or the divider announces ~8h over a route the
badges beneath it plainly show as four.

## The ETAs now move as the day is worked

`js/worklist.js`, `tests/pace-live.test.mjs`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `AGENTS.md`

Also asked for: *"as the day progresses, every time I complete an order the ETA should get
updated for the next remaining metres in the route."* `applyTodayAnchor` already ran after
every logged stop, but two things silently stopped it landing.

**The write guard was blind to a changed time.** It skipped any order whose `order`, `day`
and `scheduledDate` were unchanged — safe only while the day's clock was a constant. With
a clock that moves, a stop can sit at the same position on the same date and still be due
at a different time, and the guard threw away exactly that write. It compares
`scheduledEta` now.

**And the drive-out was measured from the depot all day.** `estimateTravel` anchored on
the cached crew start, so after finishing stop 5 the ETA for stop 6 was priced as a drive
from the muster point. It anchors on `lastDonePin` — the pin of the most recently
completed order, by `updatedAt` — falling back to the crew start. Deliberately not a GPS
fix: this is free, needs no permission, and works with the radio off. Gated on
`planDay() === localDate()`, because an evening plan for tomorrow starts at the depot like
any morning does.

## Verification

`node --test "tests/*.test.mjs"` — 474/474. New coverage: the day-1 clock moving Day 1
while days 2+ hold at 08:15, an omitted override reproducing the old schedule exactly, a
late start that reports lateness instead of resizing the day, the target-vs-route
denominator with both shortfalls, a day that beats its target, and the wiring assertions
for the cache refresh ordering and the estimate's origin.

**Not done here:** the headless-browser pass in `VERIFY.md` §3. This session's sandbox
refused to launch a local server or Edge, so the DOM-level behaviour of the pace card is
covered by source assertions rather than a screenshot. Worth a real two-phone run in the
field — check the Settings ▸ name match first.
