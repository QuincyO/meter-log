# 2026-11-13 — The photo bot takes reassigned orders off the worklist

## Orders that left the handheld can be removed from Telegram

**The problem:** work orders are reassigned and added on the handheld through the day.
The bot only ever added. A ✅ Send queued the numbers that were new and skipped the ones
already on the worklist, so an order taken off the handheld stayed on the worklist until
someone deleted it by hand on the phone.

**What shipped:** a ✅ Send of photos now also asks the Sheet which pending orders were
not in them. The bot lists those orders by number, address and appointment, with a
**🗑 Remove** button. Nothing is removed until it is tapped.

- `/keep 902732` holds an order back from the removal first.
- `/undo` puts the last removal back, with its address, map pin and appointment.
- Removed orders leave the phone on its next **⇩ Download**. No phone update is needed.
- If half the list or more is missing, the message leads with a warning that a photo
  was probably missed.
- A batch of typed numbers only is never compared, and an order logged between the
  list and the tap is left alone.

**How it works:** `queueWorklistOrders` takes `compare: true` and returns `missing` and
`pendingCount`. The new `removeWorklistOrders` action writes each order to a new
`WorklistRemoved` tab and then deletes it from `Worklist`; an order still waiting in
`WorklistInbox` is cancelled instead. When a removed work-order number is queued again,
`claimWorklistInbox` builds the new row from the archived details. `/undo` is that same
re-queue. The nightly `clearDoneWorklistJob` drops archive rows older than 30 days.

**The trap worth remembering:** a plain delete does not work here. The phone replaces
its whole `Worklist` slice after every logged stop, so any phone that has not
downloaded pushes the order straight back, and the crew runs two phones. So each
`WorklistRemoved` row is also a tombstone, and `saveWorklist` drops any incoming order
whose row id is listed there. Three details follow from that:

- The tombstone is the **row id**, not the work-order number. A restored or re-typed
  order gets a fresh id, so it is never caught by an old removal.
- A restore goes through the inbox, like any add. A row appended straight into
  `Worklist` is wiped by the next log.
- The 🗑 button carries a token. A button from an earlier send, or from before the bot
  restarted, answers "out of date" instead of removing against an old list.

**Verified:** the new tests failed before the change and pass after; the full suite
passes. The spine paths are run against the real `Code.gs` over an in-memory sheet:
compare, remove, a stale phone's push, restore, inbox cancel and the sweep. The real
`bot.mjs` was also run through a scripted conversation (photo, ✅ Send, `/keep`, 🗑, a
stale push, the old button again, `/undo`, a typed-only send) against the real `doPost`
and `doGet`, with Telegram, Ollama and the Sheet faked. Nothing was sent to the live
bot or the production Sheet, so the first real removal is the owner's to try.
